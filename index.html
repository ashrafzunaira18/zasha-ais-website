International Standards

How AQL 2.5 Protects Your Brand

We inspect under ISO 2859-1 (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) General Inspection Level II. This statistical sampling system guarantees that the pieces examined accurately represent the defect rate of the entire manufacturing batch.

Critical (0.0) Zero tolerance. Needles, broken metal, or safety violations trigger instant batch failure.
Major (2.5) Open seams, sizing variance, or stains causing customer returns.
Minor (4.0) Minor cosmetic issues (loose untrimmed thread, slight label tilt).

Everything international buyers need to know before booking.

How quickly can your inspector be at the factory?

We guarantee auditor presence anywhere in Pakistan within 48 hours of order confirmation. For major textile hubs like Lahore, Sialkot, Faisalabad, and Karachi, next-day deployment is frequently available.

How do you prevent factory bribery or corruption?

Integrity is our foundational principle. All Zasha.AIS inspectors operate under strict anti-bribery policies, rotate factory allocations, use encrypted GPS-tagged photo reporting, and sign severe zero-tolerance penalty clauses.

What payment methods do you accept from foreign buyers?

We support smooth international payments via Wise (TransferWise), Bank Wire Transfer (SWIFT), Western Union, and Payoneer. Invoicing in USD, GBP, EUR, or CAD.

What happens if my order FAILS the inspection?

If defects exceed AQL limits, the report is marked FAILED / PENDING CLIENT REVIEW. We immediately provide actionable recommendations for factory rework, replacement of defective units, or renegotiation before container stuffing is approved.

Can you help us find certified apparel manufacturers in Pakistan?

Yes! In addition to quality inspection, Zasha.AIS provides professional buying support and supplier sourcing. We maintain an extensive network of verified, audited manufacturers for knits, denim, home textiles, sportswear, and leather goods.
