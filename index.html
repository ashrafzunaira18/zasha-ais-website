Buyer Assurance & Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding dispatch times, AQL protocols, tech packs, and deliverables.

We guarantee nationwide dispatch within 24 to 48 hours. With senior auditors stationed locally across Lahore, Karachi, Sialkot, and Faisalabad, our team can reach your supplier's factory floor without logistical delays.
We adhere strictly to ISO 2859-1 (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) Level II Normal Sampling. Our standard acceptable quality limits are AQL 0.0 for Critical defects, AQL 2.5 for Major defects, and AQL 4.0 for Minor defects, or any customized tolerance specified by your brand.
Yes, absolutely. You can upload your buyer tech packs, POM (Point of Measure) tolerance charts, packaging manuals, and Golden sealed reference photos through our Client Portal or via email under strict non-disclosure terms.
We deliver the complete, verified PDF inspection dossier within 24 hours of the inspection conclusion. In urgent export scenarios, our lead auditor sends a same-day provisional flash summary via WhatsApp within 2 hours of leaving the mill.
Our auditor immediately issues an on-site Corrective Action Request (CAR) signed by the factory management, detailing specific rework protocols. We can schedule a re-inspection to verify 100% defect remediation prior to container loading.
