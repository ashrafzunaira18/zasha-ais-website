About Company

Pakistan-Based Apparel Inspection & Sourcing Services

Zasha.AIS is a Pakistan-based Apparel Inspection services company dedicated to helping global brands manage quality, compliance, and production with confidence. Service Catalogue

Our Services

End-to-end quality control checkpoints from raw materials to final shipping container loading across all 21 specialized workflows.

01

1. Pre-Shipment Inspection

Final quality check conducted when production is 100% completed and goods are packed. Inspectors randomly select finished units using international AQL standards to ensure export-ready compliance.

02

2. Final Random Inspection

Performed when at least 80–100% of the order is packed. We randomly sample cartons under AQL standards to assess measurements, stitching, labeling, and packing consistency before dispatch.

03

3. During Production Inspection

Carried out when 20–60% of garments are in stitching or finishing. Assesses the active production line and workmanship to resolve defects early before bulk production finishes.

04

4. Initial Production Check

Conducted at the start of manufacturing when the initial batch is ready. Examines fabric quality, cutting accuracy, and initial stitching standards so production starts in the right direction.

05

5. Sample Picking Inspection

Randomly selects garment samples from the batch using AQL sampling methods. Accurately predicts the quality of the entire lot without having to individually inspect every piece.

06

6. Container Loading Check

Supervises the loading process: verifies carton counts, labeling, container hygiene, and secure stacking. Ensures your shipment is sealed and dispatched without in-transit damage.

07

7. Seam and Stitching Inspection

Evaluates stitch density (SPI), thread tension, seam alignment, and defect presence such as skipped or broken stitches. Prevents structural failures and ensures premium durability.

08

8. Finishing Process

Ensures garments are clean, well-ironed, and presentable before packing. Checks thread trimming, stain removal, hangtags, and uniform folding across the entire batch.

09

9. Fabric Inspection

Examines fabric rolls for GSM, holes, slubs, crease lines, knitting lines, and oil stains. Stops defective material from entering cutting lines to protect garment consistency.

10

10. Sampling and Testing

Coordinates laboratory and on-site testing including color fastness, shrinkage, tensile strength, and pull-force checks to meet buyer physical and chemical compliance specs.

11

11. Quality Control Measures

Implements systematic monitoring guidelines across fabric, stitching, trims, and finishing. Follows standardized procedures to identify deviations early in production.

12

12. Raw Material Verification

Thoroughly inspects incoming fabrics, trims, zippers, buttons, and packaging materials against tech packs before manufacturing begins, preventing substandard inputs.

13

13. Production Process Evaluation

Assesses workflow efficiency, stitching lines, and operator handling procedures. Identifies bottlenecks and process deviations early to maintain a smooth, controlled flow.

14

14. Dyeing/Printing Inspection

Checks color accuracy, shade variation, print registration, and rubbing fastness properties. Guarantees clean, vibrant colors that do not bleed or crack after washing.

15

15. Weaving/Knitting Inspection

Identifies fabric construction defects including missed yarns, thick/thin places, slubs, and uneven tension before goods move into cutting and sewing.

16

16. Spinning and Yarn Formation

Assesses yarn strength, twist multipliers, texture, and uniformity from raw fiber drawing. Ensures yarns are defect-free and ready for subsequent weaving or knitting.

17

17. Communication with Partners

Maintains regular updates between foreign buyers, suppliers, and factory managers to address technical questions, clarify specifications, and prevent production delays.

18

18. Supplier Assessment

Evaluates factory infrastructure, production capacity, machine maintenance, and labor compliance to reduce buyer sourcing risks before order sign-off.

19

19. VOC (Verification of Conformity)

Confirms finished products comply with international regulatory codes, fiber labeling standards, and destination country safety criteria prior to shipping.

20

20. Documentation Review

Reviews packing lists, commercial invoices, bill of lading counts, certificates, and Final Inspection Reports (FRI) to ensure error-free export paperwork.

21

21. Quality Control Points

Identifies and monitors critical inspection nodes at every manufacturing stage, catching quality defects early to guarantee zero defective batches.
